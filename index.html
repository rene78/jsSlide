Endpoints are anchors. Slide only corrects interior vertices, so the blue squares never move — they define where the line starts and ends.

Surface score is the average brightness under the line; it should climb and then flatten out. Delta below the threshold ends the run.

Stopped by tells you which exit fired. Score plateaued is the normal one — but the score is an average, so a few laggard vertices can still sit off the trail while the mean looks flat. Hit Slide again to chain another pass from the result; each pass gets at least Min loops iterations regardless of how flat the score is.

The gradient measured per cell toggle exists because the original Go applies the step per metre, which only behaves when a cell is about a metre. This tile is 2.39 m/px and the step falls off with the square of that — 5.7× too slow to converge. Measuring per cell keeps it size-free.
